About RoboCodex

RoboCodex is where I put everything I wish someone had handed me: videos, written guides, calculators, and a curated list of the tools and docs actually worth using. No fluff, no gatekeeping — just what works.

What this is

A growing FTC resource built around the RoboCodex channel — a video library, readable-inline guides, interactive tools, and a curated directory of official docs and community projects for students, mentors, and families.

What it tries to do well

Give people a clearer place to start. Simple layouts, practical tone, official sources where they exist, and the lessons spelled out instead of assumed.

Who it's for

Anyone learning FTC — rookies who don't know where to start, teams trying to make their autonomous not embarrassing, and students who'd rather read than rewind a video.

How it started

The short origin story

I spent eight years in FIRST LEGO League before moving up to FTC — the same path a lot of kids take, except nobody handed me a map. I learned the control system, encoders, PID, CAD, and autonomous the hard way: one season at a time.

RoboCodex started as the notes I kept writing anyway. The channel turned those notes into videos, and this site is where the notes live in full — organized, searchable, and free, the way I wanted it when I was starting.

It's not a complete curriculum and I'm not pretending it is. It's a growing set of things that actually worked for us, passed along so the next team doesn't have to reinvent it.
