About RoboCodex

This project is being shaped as a practical FTC resource site that combines videos, starter guides, engineering tools, and curated references in one place.

Mission

RoboCodex makes robotics easier to explore by connecting original videos with useful FTC guides, simple calculators, and reliable sources.

What this site includes

A growing library of videos, beginner-friendly FTC guides, engineering calculators, and a curated resources directory.
