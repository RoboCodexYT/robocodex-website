About RoboCodex

RoboCodex started as a simple idea: make FTC and robotics feel more approachable. The goal is to bring videos, practical guides, engineering tools, and trustworthy resources into one place without pretending the site is a complete curriculum.

What this project is

RoboCodex is a growing FTC resource site built around the RoboCodex channel. It includes a video library, beginner-friendly guides, calculators, and curated references for students, mentors, and families.

What it tries to do well

It aims to give people a clearer place to start. That means showing useful content in a simple layout, pointing to official sources where possible, and keeping the tone practical rather than overly polished or promotional.
