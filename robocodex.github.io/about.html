About RoboCodex

Created by Jose, FTC Team Captain & Nationals Winner

Hi, I'm Jose

I'm a freshman at Scenic Hills Christian Academy in San Antonio, Texas. I've been doing robotics since FLL, and I just competed at FTC nationals with my team Happy Hawks (Team 24813). We won the INSPIRE Award.

This channel exists because FLL is ending. Thousands of students just like you are confused about 'what's next?'

I created RoboCodex to answer the questions I had when I was making the transition. Hopefully it helps you too.
